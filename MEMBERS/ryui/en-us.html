002 : RUI SACHIKO

Sachiko Rui, also known from initials S.R or streamer name "angelryuui" or alias "Ryui", is a Filipino VSinger and VTuber, who's target audience are people that are depressed to have at least a small will to live another day. His main goals are to save people who want to unalive and make them live on for more days, and to heal those that were traumatized from past events and bring a smile to everyone's face.
